These six real product photos were pulled from KuKirin's official store
and cross-fade automatically (with the little dot controls underneath).

Swap any of them:
  - Replace any scooter-N.jpg with your own photo, keeping the same filename.
  - The gallery auto-rotates through however many of the six are present,
    so you can drop in one or all six.

If none are present, the cover shows a clean placeholder instead of a broken image.

Tips:
  - Square-ish photos on a plain background look best in the square frame.
  - Keep each light (~1000x1000, under ~1 MB works great).
